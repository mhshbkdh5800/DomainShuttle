## 🎬 Running Your First Project

1. Double-click the DomainShuttle shortcut on your desktop.
2. Wait for the main window to open. The software performs a quick check of your system resources upon the first startup.
3. Locate the "Import Subject" button at the top left of the screen.
4. Choose the image of the object or person you want to include in your video.
5. Enter a text description in the prompt box. Describe what action you want the subject to perform.
6. Adjust the "Fidelity" slider if you want the output to stay very close to your original image.
7. Adjust the "Editability" slider if you want more creative changes during the video generation.
8. Press the "Generate" button. The software processes your request.
9. Watch the progress bar at the bottom of the window.
10. Once the bar reaches 100%, the video plays in the preview window.
11. Click the "Save" icon to export your finished video file to your computer.

## 🛠️ Resolving Common Issues

If the software does not work, check these common fixes:

- Ensure your graphics drivers are current. Visit the website of your graphics card manufacturer to find the latest updates.
- Check that you have enough space on your hard drive. If the drive is full, the system cannot save the temporary files needed for video rendering.
- Verify your GPU configuration. This software requires an NVIDIA card with support for CUDA. If you use a laptop with two graphics cards, go to your Windows Display Settings and set the "Graphics Performance" preference for DomainShuttle to "High Performance."
- Close other demanding programs before you run a generation task. Video creation uses significant CPU and GPU power.
- Restart your computer if the application hangs unexpectedly. This clears temporary memory locks that might hinder performance.

## 📖 Understanding the Technology

DomainShuttle separates your input image from the text instructions. This method allows the software to focus on the shape and texture of your subject while shifting the background and motion based on your words. This keeps your subject consistent even when the scene changes throughout the video. The software uses pre-trained models to understand shapes and motions, which speeds up the time it takes to build your video. 

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

This software runs locally on your machine. You do not need to upload your images or videos to a server. Your data stays on your hard drive unless you choose to share the exported files yourself. We do not track your usage data or collect personal information during the generation process. All processing happens within your computer's own memory and hardware.
